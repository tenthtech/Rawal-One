## Presentation and reuse

The two homepage galleries share all four photographs and change only when a visitor uses the previous or next controls. The hero opens with Lalkurti; the community gallery opens with Ayub Park. Service photography supplies place context, rather than claiming to show a service being delivered. Responsive crops preserve the scene's defining features: the Lalkurti street perspective and lights, Ayub Park's fountain and lake, Murree Road's street and Metrobus infrastructure, and the railway station's platform and arched walls. Each active photograph has a place caption and descriptive alt text; duplicate credit thumbnails have empty alt text beside their full titles and credits.

These are photographs of real places at the dates above, not depictions of the site's fictional demonstration events, notices or current operations. No photographer or pictured person's endorsement is implied. The photo licences apply to the photographs and their adaptations; they do not relicense unrelated website code or content. Share or reuse the adapted CC BY-SA images with their creators, sources, licence links and change notices, under their respective ShareAlike licences. The railway station photograph is available under CC0 1.0; acknowledging its creator is appreciated.

No generated or stock photographs are used. The only two new photographs added for this refinement are the verified Murree Road and railway station images above.
